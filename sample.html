Village of Ashton, Lee County, Illinois. Population about 929 (U.S. Census Bureau estimate for July 1, 2025; source).

Seven programs a small Illinois village can apply to, in deadline order. On Nov 2, 2026 we checked every amount, deadline, match and eligibility rule below against the program's official notice. This is a screening list, not a ruling on eligibility. Confirm the details with the agency before you apply.

Fixed deadlines

DCEO Tourism Attraction Grant Program

Deadline
Nov 13, 2026, 5:00 PM
Award
$15,000 to $200,000 per award ($1.8 million total, 9 to 20 awards expected)
Match
At least 50% of total project costs for a municipality
Who can apply
Municipalities, counties, other local governments, local promotion groups, nonprofits and businesses
For
Developing or improving a tourism attraction that is open to the public at least 100 days a year and marketed to visitors from more than 50 miles away
How
Apply through DCEO's online application form (NOFO)

OJJDP FY 2026 Opioid Affected Youth Initiative

Deadline
Grants.gov by Nov 16, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET, then the full application in JustGrants by Nov 23, 2026, 8:59 p.m. ET
Award
Up to $750,000 ($8,920,000 total, 12 awards expected)
Match
None
Who can apply
Includes cities, townships and other units of local government, which the NOFO says include villages
For
Prevention, intervention and treatment for children, youth and families affected by opioid, stimulant and other substance use disorders, working with law enforcement, schools, health and child welfare providers
Heads-up
The NOFO said to start SAM.gov registration or renewal by Oct 16, 2026, and no later than Oct 30, 2026 (NOFO)

Illinois CPRG Stretch Code Adoption Grant, Round 4

Deadline
Rolling through Dec 31, 2026, while funds last
Award
Up to $200,000 per municipality, or up to $1,000,000 for a group of five or more ($6,500,352 total)
Match
None
Who can apply
Cities, villages and incorporated towns
For
Work toward the Illinois Stretch Energy Code. You don't have to commit to adopting it, but you must make a good-faith effort to consider it, and you need base codes that support it or a plan to update them.
How
GATA pre-qualification, then apply on Euna/AmpliFund with Attachment A

Open until funds run out

DCEO CDBG Community Revitalization Program

Deadline
No fixed due date. First come, first served until funds are used up.
Award
Up to $1,000,000 for sidewalks, rehab of a publicly owned commercial building, non-residential historic preservation, or reuse of a closed building. Demolition: $250,000 (one building), $1,000,000 (four or more adjacent buildings), $2,000,000 (large structures). $13 million total.
Match
Not required. Up to 10 bonus points if you add other funding.
Who can apply
For all activities except demolition, only cities, villages, townships and counties; municipalities must not be HUD entitlement communities (Ashton and Lee County are not on the NOFO's entitlement list)
Before you apply
Meet a HUD national objective (such as benefit to low- and moderate-income people), hold a public hearing, pass a board resolution of support, and be GATA pre-qualified. Limit: one demolition and one other application per year. (NOFO)

Illinois EPA Unsewered Communities Planning Grant, Round 3

Deadline
Open until funding is used up
Award
Up to $30,000 if your service area's median household income is below the state average, dropping in steps to $17,500 at 140% to 149.9%. Not eligible at 150% or more. ($1 million total, 33 to 50 awards.)
Match
The listing doesn't mention a match; confirm with IEPA
Who can apply
Local government units, including municipalities and townships
For
A project plan to solve wastewater collection or treatment needs where service is missing or inadequate (not design or construction)
How
GATA pre-qualification, then apply on AmpliFund

USDA Community Facilities Direct Loan & Grant Program

Deadline
Ongoing, all year (Oct 1 to Sep 30)
Award
Low-interest direct loans, grants, or both. A grant can cover up to 75% of eligible costs in a community of 5,000 or fewer whose service area's median household income is below the poverty line or 60% of the state non-metro median, whichever is higher. Lower tiers are 55%, 35% and 15%. Grants depend on available funds.
Who can apply
Public bodies, nonprofits and tribes in rural areas, including villages, with no more than 20,000 residents, that can't finance the project themselves or through commercial credit at reasonable terms
For
Essential community facilities and equipment, such as town halls, street improvements, fire departments, fire trucks and public works vehicles
How
Start by contacting USDA Rural Development's Illinois office

USDA Water & Waste Disposal Loan & Grant Program

Deadline
Ongoing, all year (Oct 1 to Sep 30)
Award
Long-term, low-interest loans of up to 40 years. If funds are available, a grant may be added to keep user costs reasonable.
Who can apply
Most state and local government entities serving rural areas and towns of 10,000 or fewer, if they can't get commercial credit on reasonable terms
For
Drinking water, sewer, solid waste and storm water systems
How
Apply through RD Apply or your local USDA Rural Development office
