Before you apply: readiness checklist

  • ☐ GATA pre-qualification: Applicants must be GATA pre-qualified. Register each year in the Illinois GATA Grantee Portal (https://grants.illinois.gov/portal/). Every Illinois state grant (DCEO, IEPA, IDNR, OSFM, ICJIA, IFA) checks it. Needed for 9 of the grants above.
  • ☐ SAM.gov registration and UEI: Active SAM.gov registration with a Unique Entity ID (https://sam.gov). Renew every 12 months; new registrations can take several weeks. Needed for 9 of the grants above.
  • ☐ Grants.gov / JustGrants accounts: A Grants.gov account with an authorized organization representative (https://www.grants.gov). DOJ grants also need JustGrants (https://justicegrants.usdoj.gov): Grants.gov first, then the full application in JustGrants. Needed for 7 of the grants above.
  • ☐ NFIRS / NERIS incident reporting (fire departments only): Only if you run a fire department: keep incident reporting current in NFIRS / NERIS (https://www.usfa.fema.gov/nfirs/).
  • ☐ Hazard mitigation plan: A FEMA-approved hazard mitigation plan your board has adopted (your county's multi-jurisdictional plan counts if you joined it). Required for FEMA mitigation project grants. Not needed for anything on your list right now.
