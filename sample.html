Deadline Sweep sample report: Village of Ashton (checked Mon, Nov 2, 2026; corrected Wed, Nov 4, 2026)

Village of Ashton, Lee County, Illinois. Population 929 (Census 2025 estimate; source).

Six programs a small Illinois village can apply to, in deadline order. On Mon, Nov 2, 2026 we checked every amount, deadline, match and eligibility rule below against the program's official notice. This is a screening list, not a ruling on eligibility.

Eligibility is decided by the funding agency; check the notice before applying.

Corrected Wed, Nov 4, 2026: the USDA Community Facilities entry now lists low-interest direct loans only. We removed the DCEO CDBG Community Revitalization entry, projected program totals and award counts, and a SAM.gov registration date that has passed.

Fixed deadlines

DCEO Tourism Attraction Grant Program

Deadline
Fri, Nov 13, 2026, 5:00 PM
Award
$15,000 to $200,000 per award
Match
At least 50% of total project costs for a municipality
Who can apply
Municipalities, counties, other local governments, local promotion groups, nonprofits and businesses
For
Developing or improving a tourism attraction that is open to the public at least 100 days a year and marketed to visitors from more than 50 miles away
How
Apply through DCEO's online application form (NOFO)

OJJDP FY 2026 Opioid Affected Youth Initiative

Deadline
Grants.gov by Mon, Nov 16, 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET, then the full application in JustGrants by Mon, Nov 23, 2026, 8:59 p.m. ET
Match
None
Who can apply
Includes cities, townships and other units of local government, which the NOFO says include villages
For
Prevention, intervention and treatment for children, youth and families affected by opioid, stimulant and other substance use disorders, working with law enforcement, schools, health and child welfare providers
How
Apply on Grants.gov, then submit the full application in JustGrants (NOFO)

Illinois CPRG Stretch Code Adoption Grant, Round 4

Deadline
Rolling through Thu, Dec 31, 2026, while funds last
Award
Up to $200,000 per municipality, or up to $1,000,000 for a group of five or more
Match
None
Who can apply
Cities, villages and incorporated towns
For
Work toward the Illinois Stretch Energy Code. You don't have to commit to adopting it, but you must make a good-faith effort to consider it, and you need base codes that support it or a plan to update them.
How
GATA pre-qualification, then apply on Euna/AmpliFund with Attachment A

No fixed deadline

Illinois EPA Unsewered Communities Planning Grant, Round 3

Deadline
Open until funding is used up
Award
Up to $30,000 if your service area's median household income is below the state average, dropping in steps to $17,500 at 140% to 149.9%. Not eligible at 150% or more.
Who can apply
Local government units, including municipalities and townships
For
A project plan to solve wastewater collection or treatment needs where service is missing or inadequate (not design or construction)
How
GATA pre-qualification, then apply on AmpliFund

USDA Community Facilities Direct Loan & Grant Program

Deadline
Rolling. Applications are accepted on an ongoing basis from October 1 to September 30.
Award
Low-interest direct loans (repayment up to 40 years, never longer than the facility's useful life)
Who can apply
Public bodies, nonprofits and tribes in rural areas, including villages, with no more than 20,000 residents, that can't finance the project themselves or through commercial credit at reasonable terms
For
Essential community facilities and equipment, such as town halls, street improvements, fire departments, fire trucks and public works vehicles
How
Start by contacting USDA Rural Development's Illinois office

USDA Water & Waste Disposal Loan & Grant Program

Deadline
Rolling. Applications are accepted on an ongoing basis from October 1 to September 30.
Award
Long-term, low-interest loans (up to 40-year payback, based on the useful life of the facilities financed)
Who can apply
Most state and local government entities serving rural areas and towns of 10,000 or fewer, if they can't get commercial credit on reasonable terms
For
Drinking water, sewer, solid waste and storm water systems
How
Apply through RD Apply or your local USDA Rural Development office
