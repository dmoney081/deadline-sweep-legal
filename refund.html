Deadline Sweep Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This page explains how cancellations and refunds work for Deadline Sweep Pro ($15 per month or $150 per year). It is a plain-English summary of the refund and cancellation sections of our Terms of Service.

Cancel anytime

  • Your Pro subscription renews automatically each month or year until you cancel.
  • To cancel, email us at hello@deadlinesweep.com and ask us to cancel.
  • When you cancel, you won't be charged again. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds

  • Full refund within 14 days of your first charge. If Pro isn't for you, email us within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full, no questions asked.
  • After that 14-day window, payments are generally not refundable, including partial months or years, but you can cancel anytime to stop future charges.
  • If something went wrong (for example, a duplicate charge), or you have another reason to ask for a refund, email hello@deadlinesweep.com. We review each request individually and will reply by email.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and can take several business days to appear.

Contact

Questions about billing, cancellations or refunds? Email hello@deadlinesweep.com.
